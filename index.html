Full-Screen Standalone App & Offline Mode

Install Super Study to Home Screen

Seamless Study Without the Browser Window

Launch Super Study directly from your Home Screen or Dock in its own dedicated full-screen window—with zero browser tabs, no URL bar, and 100% offline access to all NCERT Class 11 & 12 reactions, mechanisms, trends, and offline NEET questions.

iPhone & iPad (Safari) Your Device
  1. Tap the Share button ⎋ in Safari’s bottom or top bar.
  2. Scroll down the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add in the top-right corner to install the standalone app icon.
Android (Chrome / Edge) Your Device
  1. Tap the one-click Install prompt, or tap the browser menu ⋮ in the top-right corner.
  2. Select Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm to place Super Study directly on your Home Screen.
Desktop (Chrome, Edge & macOS Safari) Your Device
  1. Chrome / Edge: Click the Install Super Study icon inside the right edge of the address bar.
  2. Safari on Mac: Click File → Add to Dock… from the top menu bar.
Offline study cache active — reactions, mechanisms & drills work even without internet.
